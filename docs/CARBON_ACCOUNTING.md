# Biogenic-carbon accounting

A cradle-to-gate GWP for photosynthetic biomass is not a single number. It is a
gross value plus a convention. This document states the conventions AlgaMetrix
implements, what each one credits, and why the two that look similar are not.

Generated evidence: `results/carbon_accounting.txt`, regenerated by
`python reproduce.py --only carbon`.

## 1. The six quantities that must travel together

For every scenario the engine reports:

1. **gross cradle-to-gate GWP** — all upstream burdens, before any biogenic adjustment;
2. **biogenic carbon incorporated** into the biomass that leaves the gate, as CO₂;
3. **carbon supplied** to the culture and the resulting **utilization efficiency**;
4. **carbon-supply upstream burden** — producing, capturing or mining the carbon feed;
5. the **adjustment** applied under the declared convention (≤ 0);
6. **net cradle-to-gate GWP** = gross + adjustment.

`LCAResult` carries all of them (`gwp_gross_kg_co2eq_per_kg`,
`biogenic_adjustment_kg_co2eq_per_kg`, `carbon_accounting_mode`,
`carbon_supply_gwp_kg_co2eq_per_kg`), so a net-negative result can never be
reported without the gross value that produced it.

## 2. The modes

`algametrix.models.CarbonAccounting`:

| mode | what it credits |
|---|---|
| `no_biogenic_credit` | nothing; gross = net |
| `source_specific_credit` | CO₂-fed carbon only, quantified as the CO₂ fixed by the **gross biomass cultivated** |
| `temporary_storage_credit_at_gate` | the carbon that actually **leaves the gate inside the product**, whatever fed it |
| `custom` | `custom_biogenic_credit_fraction` × the at-gate carbon |

`source_specific_credit` is the default because it reproduces the behaviour of
earlier versions, so published results stay reproducible. The legacy master
switch `count_biogenic_uptake=False` still forces `no_biogenic_credit` and always
wins over the mode.

### 2.1 A discrepancy the modes make visible

`source_specific_credit` credits `co2_fixed_per_kg`, which includes the carbon
fixed by biomass that is then **lost during harvesting**. That carbon never
leaves the gate in the product. `temporary_storage_credit_at_gate` credits only
the carbon in the product. The two differ by the harvesting recovery, and
`results/carbon_accounting.txt` prints the difference for every scenario rather
than resolving it silently. Which is correct depends on what is assumed to happen
to the lost biomass — a boundary question, not a modelling detail.

## 3. The three carbon feeds

### CO₂-fed
Three separable things: the **upstream burden** of producing, capturing,
purifying and delivering the CO₂ (`co2_supply_gwp`, default 0.10 kg CO₂-eq/kg for
captured flue gas, ≈0.8 for purchased liquid); the carbon **physically
incorporated**; and whether that incorporation is **credited**. The first is
always counted. The third is a convention.

### Bicarbonate-fed (NaHCO₃, e.g. *Arthrospira*)
Earlier versions credited nothing here and justified it as "avoiding double
counting". That is a conclusion presented as a derivation, and it is not stated
that way any more. What is actually true:

* the NaHCO₃ **production burden** (≈0.87 kg CO₂-eq/kg) is an upstream flow,
  counted in the gross value;
* the carbon **incorporated** into the biomass is a different flow;
* whether the second is credited is a **boundary convention**.

Both conventions are reported side by side. Under
`temporary_storage_credit_at_gate` a bicarbonate-fed system receives the same
at-gate credit as a CO₂-fed one; under `source_specific_credit` it receives none.
The reader chooses, and sees what the choice costs.

### Heterotrophic
Carbon arrives in the organic substrate. Tracked and reported separately:
carbon in the substrate (as CO₂), the substrate's **production burden**
(`substrate_gwp`, default 0.75 kg CO₂-eq/kg glucose), the carbon **incorporated**
into the product, and the fact that any downstream release of that carbon lies
**outside** the cradle-to-gate boundary. `source_specific_credit` credits nothing
for a heterotrophic system.

The substrate carbon fraction used for this bookkeeping is glucose stoichiometry
(6 × 12.011 / 180.156 = 0.400), a chemical constant, not a fitted value. It does
not enter any cost or impact sum.

## 4. Ratios

Spread statistics over GWP obey one rule: **a ratio is never computed across zero
or a negative value.** `paper/stats.py:compute_spread` reports the absolute range
as the primary summary, computes max/min over the strictly positive subset only,
and writes the denominator convention into the output, e.g.

```
ratio convention  : max/min over strictly positive values: gwp_pbr_industrial (153) / gwp_mckuin_nanno (1.1)
note              : 1 value(s) <= 0: ratios are computed over the 17 strictly
                    positive value(s) only, and the absolute range is the primary summary.
```

Spread statistics are recalculated on **both** bases — gross positive GWP and net
GWP under the declared convention — and both appear in
`results/carbon_accounting.txt`.

## 5. What is still unknown

No study record in `data/studies/studies.yaml` has a
`biogenic_carbon_convention`. Every published GWP in the dataset was computed
under *its own* convention, and that convention was not extracted. Two
consequences, both carried in the generated output:

* the published GWP spread mixes conventions, so the one non-positive point
  (`gwp_iceland_spirulina`, −0.54) is not comparable with the positive ones;
* a reproduction of a published GWP may be comparing two different conventions.
  `results/validation.txt` prints that caveat on every GWP row.

Extracting each source's convention is an open TODO on the affected records.

## 6. Reproducing with and without the credit

```bash
python reproduce.py --only carbon
```

writes every scenario under all three reported conventions. In code:

```python
from algametrix.models import CarbonAccounting
from algametrix.paper import carbon, reconstructions

scn = reconstructions.build("scp_protein")
scn.lcia.carbon_accounting = CarbonAccounting.NO_BIOGENIC_CREDIT
report = carbon.carbon_report(scn, "my case")
print(report.gross_gwp, report.net_declared, report.net_no_credit)
```

Tests covering CO₂-fed, bicarbonate-fed and heterotrophic scenarios
independently, plus gross-vs-net and the zero-ratio guard, are in
`tests/test_paper_analysis.py`.
