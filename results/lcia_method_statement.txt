LIFE-CYCLE ASSESSMENT - METHOD STATEMENT
========================================================================================
Background   : AlgaMetrix default background v1  (released 2026-08-27)
Declared in  : lcia.yaml
Standard     : ISO 14040:2006 Environmental management - Life cycle assessment -
               Principles and framework
Standard     : ISO 14044:2006 Environmental management - Life cycle assessment -
               Requirements and guidelines

1  Goal and scope
-----------------
  Study type      : attributional
  Modelling       : linear, process-based, steady-state, average (not marginal)
  Boundary        : cradle-to-gate
  Functional unit : 1 kg of dry biomass leaving the plant gate; in a multi-product
                    configuration, 1 kg of the designated main product.
  Reference flow  : The gross biomass, energy, carbon, nutrient, water and land flows
                    required to deliver that kilogram, taken from the shared mass-energy
                    inventory.
  Gate            : The product as it leaves the production site: dry powder, wet paste
                    or the designated main product of a downstream train, depending on
                    the scenario.
  Geography       : EU-27
                    The defaults are indicative of an average European background. They
                    are not regionalized: no factor responds to where the plant is. Grid
                    electricity alone spans two orders of magnitude between national
                    grids, so a study outside Europe must replace at least the
                    electricity, heat and fertiliser factors before its results mean
                    anything.
  Reference period: 2019-2022
                    The defaults describe a background of roughly 2019-2022. They carry
                    no trend and no scenario year: a prospective study of a 2035 plant
                    on a 2035 grid has to supply its own factors.
  Database        : none
                    No licensed LCI database (ecoinvent, GaBi, Agri-footprint) is
                    shipped, required or read. The background is the aggregated factor
                    table below. A user with a database licence is expected to replace
                    the table with values exported from it, and to record the database
                    name and version here.

  The calculation is organised in the four phases of ISO 14040 (goal and scope,
  inventory analysis, impact assessment, interpretation) and this file is the scope
  declaration ISO 14044 clause 4.2.3 asks for. Conformance itself is a property of a
  study, not of a program: a study made with AlgaMetrix conforms only if its author
  states goal and scope, replaces the defaults below with data representative of the
  system studied, and - for comparative assertions disclosed to the public - obtains the
  critical review ISO 14044 clause 6 requires. The defaults shipped here do not meet
  that condition on their own.

2  System boundary
------------------
  Inside:
    - Cultivation - carbon supply (CO2 or bicarbonate), nitrogen and phosphorus, process
      water, site land occupation, cultivation and circulation electricity
    - Harvesting and dewatering electricity
    - Drying - thermal energy and electricity, where the scenario dries
    - Downstream processing where enabled - disruption and extraction electricity,
      solvent-recovery heat, net solvent make-up
    - Declared materials and utilities, in the categories for which the scenario
      declares a factor
    - Direct nitrogen and phosphorus release to water from the un-assimilated fraction
    - Biogenic carbon fixed into the biomass, under the declared convention
  Outside:
    - Infrastructure and capital goods: Ponds, photobioreactors, fermenters, buildings,
      piping, membranes and their replacement are costed in the TEA and absent from the
      LCA. Published algal LCAs put infrastructure at a few per cent of GWP for open
      ponds and materially more for closed reactors and thin films, so the exclusion is
      not neutral for a comparison between reactor types.
    - Transport: Inbound transport of nutrients, substrate, solvent and CO2, and
      outbound transport of the product.
    - Packaging: Primary and secondary packaging of the product.
    - End-of-life and use phase: Outside a cradle-to-gate boundary by definition.
    - Treatment of the spent medium: Discharge and treatment of the residual broth are
      not modelled; only the direct nutrient release is, through the partitioning
      fractions below. A scenario that treats its effluent has to declare that treatment
      as a utility.
    - Cleaning and sterilisation chemicals: Not derived from the process. Declare them
      as materials where they matter.
    - Direct emissions other than N and P to water: No methane, nitrous oxide, ammonia
      or VOC release is modelled - not from the culture, not from the drying stack, not
      from solvent losses.
    - Labour, employee transport, laboratory and office overhead: Present in the TEA as
      cost, absent from the LCA as burden.
  Cut-off rule: structural
    No mass, energy or economic cut-off threshold is applied, and none can be: a
    threshold rule presupposes a complete inventory from which small contributors are
    then dropped, whereas the boundary here is set by what the process model represents.
    Everything under `excluded:` is absent by construction, not because it fell below a
    percentage. A study that needs a conventional cut-off statement has to establish it
    against a complete database-backed inventory, which this tool does not build.
    Where a waste-derived feed is used, the default convention is cut-off: the stream
    arrives burden-free at the fence line and only its own transport and pre-treatment
    are counted. Avoided-treatment credits apply only where the scenario declares system
    expansion, and are reported on their own line.

3  Allocation
-------------
  ISO 14044 clause 4.3.4.2 asks that allocation be avoided by subdivision or system
  expansion where possible, then be done on an underlying physical relationship, and
  only then on another relationship such as economic value.
  Step 1 - avoid
    Single-product scenarios allocate nothing: the whole burden goes to the product.
    Waste-derived feeds may be handled by system expansion, which is the ISO-preferred
    route, and the credit is reported on its own line so it can be removed.
  Step 2 - physical
    Mass allocation between co-products, selectable per scenario.
  Step 3 - economic
    Revenue-share allocation between co-products. This is the shipped default for multi-
    product configurations, and it is step 3 of the hierarchy, not step 2. It is the
    default because mass partitioning of a fractionated biomass assigns nearly the whole
    burden to a low-value residual meal and nearly none to the pigment or lipid the
    plant exists to make. ISO permits the choice; it requires that the choice be
    declared and tested, so both bases are selectable and a study should report the
    result under each.
  Sensitivity:
    The allocation basis is a scenario setting, so re-running under mass and under
    economic allocation is the sensitivity analysis ISO 14044 clause 4.3.4.3 asks for.
    No multi-product result is allocation-free.
  Biogenic carbon:
    Four conventions are implemented, and the result always carries the gross value, the
    adjustment and the convention that produced it, so a reader can undo the credit. See
    docs/CARBON_ACCOUNTING.md.

4  Impact assessment
--------------------
  Climate change (GWP100) [kg CO2-eq]
  method  : IPCC GWP100 (characterized)
    The shipped background factors arrive already expressed in CO2-eq by their sources,
    which do not all use the same IPCC assessment report, so the vintage is mixed and
    the indicator is screening-level. The biogenic carbon term is not a characterization
    factor but a declared convention - see `conventions:` below and
    docs/CARBON_ACCOUNTING.md.
  Cumulative energy demand [MJ]
  method  : Cumulative energy demand, total primary energy with fossil and non-fossil
            pooled (characterized)
    Reported as one pooled total. Renewable, fossil and nuclear primary energy are not
    separated, so the indicator cannot be read as fossil depletion.
  Water use [m3]
  method  : none - inventory-level water consumption (inventory)
    Direct process water consumed by the foreground plus the water carried by the
    electricity factor. This is a water inventory, not a water-scarcity indicator: no
    AWARE or equivalent characterization is applied, so a cubic metre in Sicily and a
    cubic metre in Norway count the same.
  Land occupation [m2*a]
  method  : none - inventory-level occupation (inventory)
    On-site occupation of the cultivation footprint only. No land transformation, no
    occupation embodied in purchased inputs - in particular none for the agricultural
    land behind a heterotrophic sugar feedstock - and no land-use characterization
    factor.
  Marine eutrophication [kg N-eq]
  method  : ReCiPe 2016 midpoint (H), marine eutrophication (characterized)
    The direct term uses the ReCiPe reference substance, for which nitrogen released to
    water characterizes at 1 kg N-eq per kg N. What fraction of the un-assimilated
    nitrogen reaches water is an inventory assumption, not a characterization factor -
    see `inventory_assumptions:`. The upstream fertiliser term is an aggregated
    secondary value. The receiving compartment is not modelled: all released nitrogen is
    counted here and all released phosphorus under freshwater eutrophication, wherever
    the discharge actually goes, where ReCiPe would characterize by compartment.
  Freshwater eutrophication [kg P-eq]
  method  : ReCiPe 2016 midpoint (H), freshwater eutrophication (characterized)
    As above, with phosphorus to freshwater at 1 kg P-eq per kg P, and the same absence
    of a receiving compartment.
  Terrestrial acidification [kg SO2-eq]
  method  : SO2-equivalent acidification, CML / ReCiPe family (characterized)
    Every acidification factor in the table is an aggregated secondary value with no
    traceable dataset behind it. The indicator is a screening estimate, and no
    acidification result produced by AlgaMetrix has been compared against a published
    study.

  Foreground partitioning (inventory, not characterization):
    n_to_water_frac = 0.3  [fraction of un-assimilated N] (indicative)
      Share of the nitrogen the culture does not take up that is assumed to reach water.
      A scenario assumption with no evidence base. The remainder is neither emitted nor
      tracked, so nitrogen is not closed to the environment, and ammonia volatilisation
      and denitrification to N2O are not modelled.
    p_to_water_frac = 0.5  [fraction of un-assimilated P] (indicative)
      Share of the phosphorus not taken up that is assumed to reach water. As above; the
      remainder is assumed retained in sludge or soil and is not tracked further.

7  Declared limitations
-----------------------
  [aggregated-background]
    The background is aggregated at the point of characterization: each input carries
    one number per category, already in CO2-eq, MJ, SO2-eq, N-eq or P-eq. No elementary-
    flow inventory sits behind it. Contribution analysis therefore stops at the input,
    no substance-level result exists, and in the matrix formulation of the same
    calculation the characterization matrix is close to an identity mapping - it has
    almost nothing left to do. An independent matrix or Brightway run over these factors
    verifies the linear algebra and the foreground, not the characterization.
  [no-regionalization]
    No factor depends on location. Water and land are inventory totals with no scarcity
    or biome weighting, and electricity is a single average.
  [partial-coverage]
    Coverage is not uniform across categories. GWP and CED are the only columns most
    inputs fill; declared materials and utilities reach the other five categories only
    when the scenario declares those factors, and no shipped input carries a water or
    land factor except electricity for water and the site itself for land. The
    completeness check reports the gaps for each scenario rather than letting an absent
    factor read as a zero burden.
  [indicative-defaults]
    Most shipped values are literature-typical order-of-magnitude defaults, chosen so
    that the software runs out of the box, and are flagged `quality: indicative`. The
    acidification and eutrophication factors carry no traceable dataset at all. GWP
    results have been compared against published studies; the acidification,
    eutrophication, water and land results have not been compared against anything.
  [no-uncertainty-in-background]
    The factors are point values. The uncertainty layer samples them over declared
    bands, but those bands are scenario assumptions, not database uncertainty
    distributions.

  22 of 23 factors are flagged `indicative`: literature-typical values with no traceable
  dataset behind them. Replace them with data from a declared database before publishing
  a plant-specific result.
