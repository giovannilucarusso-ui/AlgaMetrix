COST INDICATORS AND BREAK-EVEN PRICE
==============================================================================
production cost, net cost, allocated cost and break-even price (Tables C and D of the manuscript)
generated by reproduce.py on 2026-10-26

DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  c_prod            production cost: annual operating cost, including
                    straight-line depreciation, over annual dry biomass. No
                    revenue is subtracted and no return on capital is added.
  c_net             net production cost: operating cost minus co-product
                    revenue and other credits, over annual dry biomass.
  allocated cost    operating cost times the product's allocation share, over
                    the product's annual mass. With 'none' the main product
                    carries all of it and no revenue is subtracted.
  p*                break-even price of the main product: NPV over the plant
                    life is zero after tax, with working capital, start-up
                    cost and year-by-year depreciation, and co-product prices
                    held fixed.
  simplified gap    C_DFC [CRF(i, n) - 1/n_dep] / (m c_prod): the gap
                    p*/c_prod - 1 that remains when tax, working capital and
                    start-up cost are left out.
  Every value is in the currency and price year of the price set its scenario
  is run with (column 'basis'). Rows on different bases are not compared with
  each other.
